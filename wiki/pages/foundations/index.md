# Foundations

The physics that every calculation in this project rests on. Suggested order:

- [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md): the equation everything approximates; the variational principle
- [Born–Oppenheimer approximation](born-oppenheimer.md): why nuclei move on an energy surface; isotopes
- [Potential energy surface](potential-energy-surface.md): minima, saddle points, vibrations, $D_e$ and $D_0$
- [Chemical and van der Waals bonds](chemical-and-van-der-waals-bonds.md): shared electrons against weak attraction between intact units, and the spectrum in between
- [Spin and multiplicity](spin-and-multiplicity.md): singlets, doublets, triplets; the parity rule
- [Units](units.md): hartree, eV, kcal/mol, cm⁻¹, and the energy scales that matter here
