# Methods

How the electronic energy is approximated, from cheap to accurate. These are
the rungs of the project's method ladder (`doc/Plan.md` §2).

Planned pages:

- Hartree–Fock and the self-consistent field
- Density functional theory and functionals (ωB97X-D4, ωB97M-V)
- MP2 perturbation theory
- Coupled cluster, CCSD(T)
- Semi-empirical tight binding (GFN2-xTB)
- Basis sets: Gaussian functions, def2 and cc-pVXZ families, diffuse functions
- Resolution of the identity (RI) approximations
