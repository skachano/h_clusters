# Pitfalls

- [Self-interaction error](self-interaction-error.md): DFT and [H₂⁺](app:s/2/1/2), [H⁻](app:s/1/-1/1), [H₅⁺](app:s/5/1/1)
- [Missing dispersion](dispersion.md): neutral van der Waals complexes
- [Missing diffuse functions](diffuse-functions.md): [H⁻](app:s/1/-1/1) unbound without them
- [Finite-basis "binding" of unbound anions](unbound-anions.md): [H₂⁻](app:s/2/-1/2) and other phantoms
- [Spin contamination](spin-contamination.md): ⟨S²⟩ and unrestricted wavefunctions
- [Quantum nuclear effects](quantum-nuclear-effects.md): when harmonic nuclei aren't enough
