# Pitfalls

Known ways a calculation gives a confident wrong answer, each paired with a
species from this project that shows it (`doc/Plan.md` §1.2).

Planned pages:

- Self-interaction error (H₂⁺ with DFT)
- Missing dispersion (neutral H₂ complexes)
- Missing diffuse functions (H⁻)
- Finite-basis false binding of unbound anions (H₂⁻)
- Spin contamination in open-shell calculations
- Quantum nuclear effects and unreliable harmonic ZPE in hydrogen
